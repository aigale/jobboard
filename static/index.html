Frequently asked

Questions from job seekers

How is this different from JobStreet or LinkedIn?

Most boards wait for employers to post. We go to each company's own careers page and list new roles within minutes, so you see them before they spread to other sites.

Do I need to pay to apply?

No. Applying is always free. Legitimate employers never charge placement or processing fees. If anyone asks you for money, report the listing and we'll remove it.

Are the remote jobs open to people living in the Philippines?

Yes. Every remote job here has been checked to confirm the company hires Philippine-based workers, either as employees or contractors.

What does "night shift" mean on a listing?

The role follows US, UK or Australian business hours. These jobs usually include a night differential of at least 10% under the Labor Code.

Can fresh graduates find jobs here?

Yes. Use the Entry level filter to see roles that accept fresh grads and career shifters, including many with paid training.
